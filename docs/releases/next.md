# Next release (post-1.0)

- Dynamic (JKL) trimming now previews the trimmed result live on the Program monitor: the monitor shows the trimmed clip's first frame (left edge) or last frame (right edge) as the edge moves. Nothing is written until K commits it as one undo step; Esc restores the project and playhead exactly. At a cut (roll or ripple with a neighbouring clip) the monitor shows Premiere-style two-up: the outgoing clip's last frame beside the incoming clip's first frame.
- Sequence pixel aspect ratio is now applied to titles, text, shapes, SVG, stickers, captions and particles in export, the Program monitor (frame shown at the display aspect, overlays and video laid out in display space) and graphic template thumbnails, so a circle stays a circle in DV NTSC (0.9091), HDV (1.333) and anamorphic (2.0) sequences. Verified on real pixels in `apps/web/e2e/sequence-par-overlays.spec.ts`.
- Sequence pixel aspect ratio, second pass: the WebGPU preview compositor now lays each layer out in display space (rotation, scale, position, anchor, then the 1/PAR squeeze), matching Canvas2D and export, so a rotated layer is no longer a parallelogram in DV NTSC, HDV or anamorphic sequences. The GPU layer matrix also now takes rotation in degrees (clockwise) and position in canvas pixels with y down, like the other renderers. Blur, glow, shadow and other effects on overlays (export) and on adjustment layers (monitor and export) run on a display-space copy of the frame, so their spread is isotropic where it is looked at. The Program monitor's CSS blur on a video clip is filtered in a display-space layer too, so a blurred clip is isotropic in the monitor as in export. Verified on real pixels in the WebGPU and software-fallback Playwright projects (`apps/web/e2e/sequence-par-geometry.spec.ts`, `fallback-sequence-par-geometry.spec.ts`).
- Project packages now embed WOFF2 fonts (as-is, when the OS/2 licence allows it) and report restricted ones, in every browser and the desktop app. Licences are read with native `DecompressionStream("brotli")` where available, the desktop shell's Node zlib bridge, or a bundled Brotli decoder (`brotli-dec-wasm`, MIT/Apache-2.0, loaded on first use as a separate ~210 KB chunk).
- The Effects and Transitions panels now preview every card on your own footage instead of decorative artwork. Each card shows a still poster of the frame under the playhead (the selected clip when the playhead is inside it, else the topmost video clip there, else the nearest video clip; a bundled neutral sample scene when the project has no video). Effect cards are rendered by the editor's own effect engines (the same canvas passes as the Canvas2D fallback renderer and export, default parameters, LUT included); transition cards blend two moments of the same footage with the real transition engine (the old amber shapes, including the Iris circle, are gone). Hovering or keyboard-focusing a card plays a looped ~2 s window (12 fps) with the effect or transition applied; leaving stops it, and `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps the poster. Chroma key shows a real key of the footage's own dominant colour (or green when the footage has a green screen) over a transparency checkerboard and Stabilize shows its stabilization crop; both carry a small badge ("Sample key", "Needs analysis") so the card is honest about what it demonstrates. Film grain is shown at 2.5x its amount because per-pixel noise averages away at card size. The footage window is decoded once at card size and shared by all cards; posters render lazily for visible cards only, one at a time in genuine idle periods, and no preview work starts while the playhead is being scrubbed or played (it resumes 300 ms after it settles). Posters live in their own 32 MB LRU and hover frames in a separate 16 MB one, rendered a few frames ahead of playback, so sweeping the pointer across cards never evicts other cards' posters. Verified on real pixels in `apps/web/e2e/effect-previews.spec.ts` (gpu) and `fallback-effect-previews.spec.ts` (no WebGPU, software).
- Transition card posters now rest on a frame that shows what the transition does: Dip to black / white, Flash Cut, Film Burn, Light Leak, Flip and the eased dissolves no longer open on a solid square or a single shot (hover still sweeps 0 to 1). The Effects chips have honest states: "No effects match" only appears for a non-empty search, Chroma key is a card in the Keying grid (with its Configure button below), and the search placeholder reflects the active chip (Audio disables it). The preview pipeline and the About, AI connection, Compress, Collaboration, shortcuts and sticker dialogs now load on first use, taking the editor chunk from 504.8 to about 484 KB gzip. Fixed a crash when switching between the Effects and Transitions panels (preview renderer disposal).
- Transition cards now blend your footage towards the next clip on the timeline (the first frame of the clip that follows the one under the playhead), or towards the bundled sample scene when the project has a single clip, so wipes, slides, pushes, splits, irises and spins each show their own geometry on a talking-head clip instead of two near-identical moments. Radial Wipe and Spin rest on a frame that tells them apart from Wipe Right and from each other, and a failed preview chunk fetch now degrades to plain cards instead of taking the panel down.
- Looks and LUTs in the Color tab now show their real result on your footage. The 30 built-in looks, imported LUTs, saved looks and the white balance presets are cards with a poster of the playhead frame graded by the same effect and colour pipeline "apply" uses (hover or keyboard focus plays it; reduced motion keeps the poster). The two LUT sections are merged into one "Looks and LUTs" section with labelled category tabs, plain-language adjustment names instead of raw effect ids, an empty state and icon buttons; a look shows the selected ring only when it is really applied. Code is lazy-loaded (editor chunk 505.0 to 500.4 KB gzip). Verified on real pixels in `apps/web/e2e/looks-luts.spec.ts` and `fallback-looks-luts.spec.ts`.
- Motion presets, text animation, emphasis, particle presets and the shader browser now preview on your own footage (the selected clip, or the sample scene when the project has none), drawn by the same engines the Program monitor and export use: the preset's real keyframes, your own selected text through the title engine, the particle sampler, and the real shader output. Cards play on hover or keyboard focus, keep a still poster for reduced motion, and a failed preview says "Preview unavailable" instead of a stand-in gradient.
- Particle and emphasis cards are representative at rest: a particle poster is the instant where the preset is most visibly at work (ambient dust and snow start in their steady state, dots are enlarged for card size and the footage is dimmed so grey dust reads), and an emphasis poster is a 2 x 2 filmstrip of real engine frames (rest, peak, trough), so Pulse, Heartbeat, Glow, Breathe and Flash are told apart without hovering. Verified on real pixels against the footage in `apps/web/e2e/inspector-previews.spec.ts`.
- Graphics > Overlays cards and every template surface now show the real thing instead of decorative art. Each overlay card (Progress bar, Letterbox 2.39, Follow callout, Frame border) is the overlay resolved from its editing template and drawn by the title / graphics engines over the footage frame at the playhead (the bundled sample scene when the project has no visual media), with a poster at rest, a short hover / keyboard-focus animation and reduced motion keeping the poster; the renderer is a lazily loaded chunk on the shared effect-preview pipeline. Making the cards honest exposed three bugs in what "apply" produced, now fixed: template shapes ignored their width / height (a letterbox bar was a small square), contrast grades were written as +0.08 instead of the 1.08 multiplier (they greyed the clip), and the Follow callout showed a literal {{label}}; the Progress bar now grows from its left edge. Every built-in template has a rendered thumbnail (the template applied through the real engines over the sample scene, committed under `public/template-thumbs`, regenerate with `apps/web/scripts/render-template-thumbs.mjs`; saved and cloud templates are rendered lazily and cached), shared by the welcome gallery, Templates tabs and preview modal through one `TemplateThumb`. Welcome cards no longer collapse the thumbnail to a chip, rows keep even heights, and platform tabs with no templates are hidden. Verified on real pixels in `apps/web/e2e/overlays-templates.spec.ts` and `fallback-overlays-templates.spec.ts`.
- Overlay and template polish, second pass: the Frame Opacity and Watermark Opacity template controls now work (template blend opacity is converted to the project's 0-100 scale and honoured by the 2D shape and text renderers in preview, export and the cards; shape transform and animation opacity is now also applied by the graphics engine on export). Overlay cards in vertical or square projects show the whole frame fitted inside the 16:9 card instead of a centre crop, lay out at card size rather than project resolution (no 4K canvases per hover frame) and share their scratch engines through a lock so concurrent cards cannot clear each other. Template text now wraps inside the frame (the Quote Card quote is no longer clipped), a failed sample-scene fetch no longer disables runtime thumbnails for the session, and a unit test fails when a built-in template changes without its bundled thumbnail being regenerated.
- Transition cards: Spin and Flip posters rest where the shot is large and legible, and the incoming shot of a transition card now plays (decoded from the next clip) while the card is hovered.

- Looks and LUTs: cards preview on top of the clip's own effects and grade exactly as apply composes them, with a Before / after split and a LUT strength control; category tabs wrap instead of clipping.
- Look, LUT, saved-look and white balance cards: one Before / after switch for the whole Color tab, a Sample badge when a card shows the bundled sample scene, and posters are stamped so tests wait for the real render.
- Particle preset cards: Soft Dissolve now emits across the frame (a visible dissolve, in the preview and on apply), Ambient Dust reads at card size, and every hover loop starts with particles already in flight.
- Overlay and template polish, third pass: Overlays and Graphics > Templates cards in vertical or square projects now place your footage exactly as the Program monitor does (contained and letterboxed on black, never cover-cropped), from the real decoded playhead frame at its own aspect instead of the small import thumbnail, so they are crisp. Overlay cards are full panel width so a 12 px progress bar is a visible few pixels. The template modal hero plays the template's own animation on hover or keyboard focus (reduced motion keeps the still). Preview SVG and sticker clips now honour blend opacity like export, stickers can take a blend mode and opacity from the inspector, and motion instances mirror their 0..1 opacity on the clip's 0-100 Blend Opacity scale. The save-template dialog plays its exit animation. Verified in real Chrome: saved and cloud template thumbnails, a 4K long-task trace (no long tasks), and an export-versus-preview pixel test for blend opacity (`blend-opacity.spec.ts`).

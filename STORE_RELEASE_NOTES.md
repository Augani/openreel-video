## Shared highlights (reference / social copy)

This release's headline additions, drawn from the shipped changes:

- **On-device smart tools** — local background removal, subject tracking, smart
  reframe, stabilization, silence cleanup, and Shorts planning where supported.
- **Pro audio export** — LUFS loudness normalization (ITU-R BS.1770) and audio-only
  export (M4A / WAV).
- **Text-to-speech voiceover** — generate narration tracks from text.
- **Crop editor (iOS)** — full-screen crop with aspect presets, draggable handles,
  pan and pinch-to-zoom.
- **Stability** — fixed trim flicker across clip types and refined clip dragging for
  frame-accurate edits.

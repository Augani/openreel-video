APP STORE DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
---------------------------------------
OpenReel Video is a full-featured video editor built for creators who want professional results on iPhone and iPad. Whether you're editing a short clip or a full production, OpenReel gives you the tools to do it without compromise.

TIMELINE EDITING
Work with unlimited video, audio, text, and graphics tracks on a multi-track timeline. Cut, trim, split, and ripple delete with frame-accurate precision. Scrub through your project in real time with Metal-powered GPU acceleration.

EFFECTS AND COLOR
Apply filters, blend modes, and video effects directly on the timeline. Fine-tune your look with color wheels, RGB curves, and HSL controls. Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, and more with live preview.

TEXT AND ANIMATION
Add titles and captions with 20+ animation presets including typewriter, fade, slide, bounce, and glitch. Customize fonts, gradients, outlines, and shadows. Import SRT or VTT caption files.

AUDIO TOOLS
Mix multiple audio tracks with per-clip volume and fade controls. Apply EQ, compression, reverb, and noise reduction. Beat detection syncs your edits to the music automatically.

ON-DEVICE SMART TOOLS
Background removal, smart reframe, motion tracking, face and body analysis, stabilization, and silence cleanup use Apple's Vision, Metal, Core Image, and media frameworks on your device. No OpenReel GPU server is required.

EXPORT
Export in ProRes or HEVC using hardware encoding. Fast, high quality, and never watermarked.

WEB APP INCLUDED
OpenReel also runs in Chrome and Edge at openreel.video. Same project format across all your devices.
